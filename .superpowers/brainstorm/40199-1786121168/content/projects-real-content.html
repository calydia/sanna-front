In progressFinnish-language book

Miksi saavutettavuus epäonnistuu organisaatioissa?

I’m writing about why accessibility so often remains a series of audits and individual fixes, even in organisations that care about it. The book looks beyond individual WCAG failures to ownership, decision-making, processes, measurement and continuity.

Why the book changes shape: it has no destination link yet, so presenting it like an available resource creates a false expectation. The full-width status panel makes “in progress” feel intentional and avoids the empty half-row in the current desktop grid.

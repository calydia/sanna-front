About Sanna

I’m Sanna Kramsi, an accessibility specialist and developer from Finland. I work with accessibility from several directions: development, accessibility evaluation, training and organisational practices.

How I think about accessibility

Part of product quality

Accessibility belongs throughout the work, not only in a check at the end.

Prevent problems returning

Finding issues matters, but so does understanding why they happen and changing how teams work.

Practical and approachable

People need enough information to make better decisions without first becoming accessibility specialists.

A11ying with Sanna

A11ying with Sanna brings together the accessibility resources I maintain, my writing, videos, talks and other projects.

The name comes from a11y. I use A11ying almost as a verb: learning, testing, improving and putting accessibility into practice.
